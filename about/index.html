Who I am

My name is Johnny. I'm French (sorry for the imperfect English), and as I'm writing this I'm 18 years old. I've been into computers since I was very young. I learned to use one around age 3, got my first one in 2015, and started making games on Scratch in 2019.

Since then I never stopped learning. I moved on from Scratch, got a newer computer and a laptop, and picked up a bit of everything: HTML, JavaScript, CSS, Lua, Python, and Haxe. I've also done some modding on the side.

I don't use generative AI in my own work. Everything I make is 100% by me.
